Red Atlas News is committed to accurate, fair and transparent journalism. This policy explains how we choose, prepare, check and correct our stories.

Accuracy and sourcing

  • We base our reporting on credible sources: established news organizations, official statements, government and central bank publications, company filings and recognized market data providers.
  • Important facts are checked against more than one source whenever possible.
  • We link to our sources so readers can verify the information.
  • We clearly separate facts from analysis. When we offer context or outlooks, we say so.

Use of AI and technology

We use technology, including AI-assisted tools, to monitor breaking news, summarize source material and help draft articles. AI does not decide what is true. Every article is reviewed by a member of our editorial team for accuracy, clarity and fairness before it is published. We do not publish fabricated quotes, and we do not present AI-generated images as real photographs of news events.

Images

We use images that we are licensed or permitted to use, such as public-domain and Creative Commons images, licensed stock photos, and our own graphics. Credits are given where required.

Independence and fairness

  • Our coverage is not influenced by advertisers, sponsors or any political group.
  • We aim to present different sides of a story fairly and avoid sensational or misleading headlines.
  • Any sponsored content will always be clearly labeled.

Market and financial information

Prices and market data shown on Red Atlas News may be delayed and are for information only. Our articles about gold, markets and the economy are not investment advice.

Corrections

When we make a mistake, we correct it quickly and openly. Significant corrections are noted at the end of the article with the date of the update. To report an error, please use our Contact page.

Updates

News develops quickly. When a story changes, we update the article and show the updated date.
