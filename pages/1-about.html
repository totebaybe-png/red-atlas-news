Red Atlas News is an independent digital newsroom covering the stories that move the world and the markets: global affairs, gold and precious metals, financial markets, business and the economy.

Our mission

We believe readers deserve news that is fast, accurate and easy to understand. Every day we track major developments around the world and explain what they mean, from geopolitical events to central bank decisions and the forces that drive the price of gold.

What we cover

  • World: the most important international news, explained with context.
  • Gold: daily gold and silver price movements, live calculators, forecasts and the factors behind them.
  • Markets: stocks, currencies, oil and the trends investors are watching.
  • Economy: inflation, interest rates, jobs and the data that shapes everyday life.
  • Business and Tech: the companies and technologies changing the world economy.

How we work

Our reporting is built on information from established news organizations, official statements, government and central bank publications, and recognized market data providers. We cite and link to our sources so readers can check the facts for themselves.

We use modern technology, including automated monitoring and AI-assisted tools, to track global developments and prepare in-depth reports. Every article passes automated fact-checking against its primary sources before publication, and we correct any errors quickly and openly. You can read more in our Editorial Policy.
