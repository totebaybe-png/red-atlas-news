Macroeconomic & Market Drivers Today

Macroeconomic market conditions present a complex background for precious metals. According to Nikkei Asia, major Asian financial institutions, including Singapore's DBS, OCBC, and UOB, have reported third-quarter earnings amid pressures on share prices as lenders navigate elevated bond yields and persistent interest rate uncertainty. The broader global yield environment has kept capital allocation tight across equity markets, providing background support for non-yielding assets such as gold.

Concurrently, festive consumer demand across South Asia has delivered significant physical volume to bullion markets. As reported by the Times of India, the All India Gem & Jewellery Domestic Council (GJC) estimated preliminary Dhanteras festive sales at approximately Rs 60,000 crore, reflecting a 15% to 20% increase in value terms compared to the previous year. While overall volume remained broadly stable, elevated nominal gold rates drove higher transaction values. Additionally, demand for diamond jewellery expanded by 25% to 30% over the period.

Financial commentators noted evolving consumer trends within the festive landscape. Dr. Shunil Tripathi, an NCFE trainer empanelled with Indian regulatory bodies, highlighted that cultural events provide structural motivation to transition domestic cash balances into investment assets, including Gold ETFs and systematic investment plans. On the commercial side, chartered accountant Balvinder Singh Oberoi noted that retail traders track festive sales closely to gauge broader balance sheet health, while PwC India retail leader Ravi Kapoor emphasized that festive spending spans multiple consumer sectors, with Tier-II and Tier-III markets contributing heavily through expanded digital commerce channels.
