How the gold price is calculated

Gold is traded worldwide in US dollars per troy ounce (31.1035 grams). To get a local price, the spot price is divided by 31.1035 to find the price per gram, converted at the live exchange rate, and multiplied by the purity of each karat. Every price on this page is cross-checked against an independent market source.

What moves gold prices

  • Interest rates: higher rates tend to weigh on gold, which pays no interest.
  • The US dollar: a weaker dollar usually lifts gold prices.
  • Inflation and uncertainty: investors often buy gold as a store of value in turbulent times.
  • Central banks: large purchases by central banks support demand.

What is the difference between 24K, 22K and 18K gold?

Karats measure purity. 24K is 99.9% pure gold, 22K is 91.67%, 21K is 87.5% and 18K is 75%. Lower karats are mixed with other metals, which makes them harder and cheaper per gram.

What is a tola, a sovereign and a tael?

They are traditional gold weights. A tola is 11.66 grams (South Asia and the Gulf), a sovereign is 8 grams (South India and Sri Lanka), and a tael is about 37.43 grams (Hong Kong).

Why is the price in gold shops higher?

Shops add making charges, a dealer premium and local taxes on top of the value of the gold itself.

What moves the price of gold?

Interest rates, the US dollar, inflation, central bank buying, geopolitical risk and investor demand are the biggest drivers of the gold price.
