Australian GST Rules on Investment Gold vs. Retail Jewellery

Tax treatment is a major factor separating investment bullion from decorative jewellery in Australia. Under Australian Goods and Services Tax (GST) legislation, precious metal qualifies as GST-free investment bullion if it is in a tradeable bar, wafer, or coin form with a fineness of at least 99.5% pure gold (which encompasses all 24K 999 and 9999 gold products). As a result, buying 24K Australian Gold Kangaroo coins, Lunar coins, or 9999 gold bars from a bullion dealer incurs zero GST.

By contrast, gold coins or medallions with a purity below the 99.5% threshold—such as historic or foreign 22K coins (91.67% fine)—do not meet the Australian Taxation Office definition of GST-free precious metal unless specific collectible or second-hand rules apply, meaning they can attract GST. That is why Australian bullion investors and SMSF trustees focus almost exclusively on 24K (9999 fine) coins and bars.

All finished gold jewellery sold at retail in Australia—whether crafted in 9K, 14K, 18K, 22K, or 24K—is classified as a taxable consumer good and includes GST in the retail price, on top of the jeweller's craftsmanship and showroom markups.

  • Gold bullion bars and coins with at least 99.5% purity (all 24K 9999 products) are exempt from GST in Australia.
  • 24K Perth Mint Kangaroo coins, Lunar coins, and 9999 cast or minted bars are completely GST-free.
  • Physical 24K bullion is widely held inside Australian Self-Managed Super Funds (SMSFs) subject to storage and audit rules.
  • Retail gold jewellery of any karat (including 22K bridal ornaments and 18K/9K rings) is subject to Australian GST.

What Moves the Gold Price in Australia: The Aussie Dollar, RBA, and Mining Output

The gold price in Australian dollars is renowned among domestic portfolio managers for acting as a natural shock absorber during global market downturns. Because the Australian dollar is a pro-cyclical commodity currency closely tied to global industrial growth and iron ore demand, the Aussie dollar often softens against the US dollar whenever global financial stress or recession fears emerge. At those exact moments, safe-haven buying typically lifts the US dollar gold price—and the weaker AUD exchange rate amplifies the gain in Australian dollar terms.

Conversely, during periods when a strong Australian dollar rallies against the greenback, local AUD gold prices may remain steady even if USD gold climbs. Therefore, tracking the live AUD gold rate directly is essential for Australian residents rather than relying solely on US dollar financial headlines.

Domestic monetary policy set by the Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA), domestic inflation expectations, real yields on Australian government bonds, and central bank bullion purchases across the Asia-Pacific region also shape the daily trend of gold in Australia.

  • The AUD/USD exchange rate acts as a natural buffer, often lifting AUD gold prices when global risk sentiment weakens.
  • Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA) interest rate decisions and local CPI inflation data influence domestic investor demand.
  • Australian gold mining companies on the ASX benchmark their domestic operating margins against the live AUD ounce price.

Is investment gold exempt from GST in Australia?

Yes. Under Australian GST law, gold bars, wafers, and bullion coins with a purity of at least 99.5% fine gold (which includes all 24K 999 and 9999 products) are GST-free. However, lower-purity coins (such as 22K coins) and all retail gold jewellery attract standard Australian GST.

Why does the gold price in Australian dollars move differently from the US dollar gold price?

The gold price in Australia is calculated by converting the global US dollar spot price into Australian dollars using the live AUD/USD exchange rate. Because the Australian dollar fluctuates against the US dollar, a weaker Aussie dollar will push the local AUD gold price higher even if the USD spot rate stays flat.

What is the difference between Perth Mint cast bars and minted bars?

Both are refined to 99.99% pure (24K) gold and backed by the Government of Western Australia. Cast bars are poured into moulds with a rustic finish and carry lower fabrication premiums over spot, while minted bars are precision-struck with a mirror finish and sealed inside tamper-evident serialized assay cards.

Can I buy physical gold inside an Australian Self-Managed Super Fund (SMSF)?

Yes. Australian SMSFs frequently invest in 24K GST-free physical gold bullion bars and coins. Trustees must ensure the bullion is titled in the SMSF's name, insured, never used personally by members, and ideally stored in an audited professional vault that provides annual holding statements.

How is 22K gold jewellery priced in Sydney and Melbourne stores?

Specialist 22K jewellers in Australia typically weigh the ornament in grams, multiply that weight by the daily 22K gold rate per gram, add a craftsmanship making charge based on design complexity, and apply Australian GST to the final retail total.

What does a 375 or 750 stamp mean on Australian gold jewellery?

Millesimal fineness stamps indicate parts per thousand of pure gold. A 375 stamp means 9K gold, 585 means 14K gold (58.5% pure), 750 means 18K gold (75% pure), 916 means 22K gold (91.67% pure), and 9999 means 24K pure bullion.
