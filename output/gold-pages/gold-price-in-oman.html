Hallmarking, Assay Standards, and Consumer Protection in the Sultanate

Oman enforces strict quality and hallmarking standards to safeguard buyers in its gold and jewellery markets. Under regulations overseen by national commerce and standardization authorities, gold jewellery and bullion sold in licensed Omani shops must be assayed and stamped with clear hallmarks confirming their exact millesimal fineness—999 for 24K, 916 for 22K, 875 for 21K, and 750 for 18K.

When shopping in Muttrah Souq, Ruwi, or modern Muscat malls, always inspect the inner surface of rings, clasps, and bangles to verify the official fineness mark and maker stamp. Before completing your transaction, insist on a formal itemized invoice displaying the shop's commercial details, the karat purity, the net weight of gold in grams, the live per-gram OMR rate on that day, the separate workmanship fee, and the weight of any non-gold stones. Keeping this official receipt guarantees authenticity and ensures hassle-free resale or exchange in the future.
