Strict Assay Hallmarking and Consumer Protection in Bahrain

Bahrain is renowned throughout the Middle East for having one of the oldest and most rigorous government assay and hallmarking systems in the region. Under strict national regulations overseen by the Ministry of Industry and Commerce, every piece of gold jewellery sold in the Kingdom must be tested and stamped by the official Bahrain assay office before it can be placed in a retail showcase.

This mandatory independent inspection is a major reason why buyers from neighboring Gulf states travel specifically to Manama to buy Bahraini gold. When examining any ornament, look for the official Bahrain hallmark alongside the numerical fineness stamp—999 for 24K, 916 for 22K, 875 for 21K, or 750 for 18K—which guarantees that the alloy meets its exact advertised purity.

Furthermore, licensed shops in Bahrain are required to weigh items on certified digital scales positioned clearly in the customer's view and issue a detailed official invoice. Always verify that your receipt records the exact karat purity, net gold weight in grams, the daily BHD per-gram rate, the separate workmanship charge, and any stone specifications.
