Krakow Shooting: 6 Dead and 15 Wounded in Market Rampage

By Red Atlas News Desk

Photo: Andrzej Otrębski / CC BY-SA 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons

A lone gunman wearing military attire opened fire from a car in Krakow's historic square before police shot and arrested him, leaving six dead and 15 injured.

A rare and devastating krakow shooting left six people dead and at least 15 others wounded on Saturday afternoon after a gunman opened fire on bystanders in the city's historic Main Market Square. Polish authorities reported that the attacker, a male Polish citizen clad in military-style fatigues, drove into the crowded pedestrian hub around 3:00 p.m. local time (14:00 GMT) and began discharging a military-grade firearm at random before engaging in a violent standoff with responding police officers.

  • Casualties: Six civilians confirmed dead (four women and two men); at least 15 people injured, including two responding police officers.
  • Location: Main Market Square in central Krakow, Poland, near Slawkowska Street, with the suspect's apprehension occurring near Dietla and Stradomska streets.
  • Suspect Details: A Polish male acting alone, dressed in military-style fatigues and armed with a military-type long gun alongside multiple weapons stored in his vehicle.
  • Police Response: Officers confronted the suspect, resulting in a gunfight where the attacker was wounded and arrested; one officer remains in critical condition after being shot three times.
  • National Impact: Prime Minister Donald Tusk and regional authorities launched a full investigation, noting that mass shooting incidents remain exceptionally rare in Poland.

Chaos in the Main Square: How the Krakow Shooting Unfolded

The tragedy erupted shortly before 3:00 p.m. local time in Krakow's Main Market Square, one of the largest medieval squares in Europe and a major hub for international tourists and locals alike. According to Krakow police spokesperson Katarzyna Cisło in an interview with TVN24, the suspect drove a dark car into the open pedestrian area near Slawkowska Street and began discharging a military-type long gun directly at people gathered in the market.

Witnesses described sudden gunfire that caused widespread panic across the crowded square. Speaking to news website Interia, a witness identified as Konrad described the terrifying onset of the assault.

"I saw shots being fired from the driver’s side window, and smoke was coming out. I ran to the police station in the main market square; when I burst inside, it was full of people hiding on the floor,"
Konrad told Interia.

Another bystander, a 67-year-old man named Julek, told Reuters that he narrowly escaped the vehicle's path.

"If we had been two minutes earlier, we might have been shot,"
Julek told Reuters, explaining that the dark vehicle appeared abruptly before the driver opened fire into a group of people and sped off.

Visitors near historical landmarks scrambled for cover as dozens of gunshots echoed across the square. Jonathan Balshaw, a visitor from Ipswich who was outside St Mary's Basilica with his Polish girlfriend Dominika, described the ordeal to BBC News.

"We ran down the side of the church and through the side door and hid with several other people until the gunfire died down. A lot of shots were fired, more than 20 with it sounding like multiple different firearms - there were two distinctly different noises,"
Balshaw told the BBC. Meanwhile, tourist Caitlin Shasby from Newcastle told BBC News that she and her friend Jon heard gunfire from their hotel room before hotel staff called instructing them to
"stay in our rooms until further notice,"
according to the outlet.

Police Confrontation, Weaponry, and Suspect Custody

Following initial emergency calls, law enforcement teams rushed to the scene to track down the fleeing vehicle. Małopolska regional police reported to TVN24 that the attacker was dressed in military-style attire and utilized a high-powered, military-type long gun during the rampage. Authorities subsequently intercepted the gunman near the intersection of Dietla and Stradomska streets, where a heavy firefight ensued.

During the confrontation, responding officers exchanged fire with the suspect. Distressing footage authenticated by BBC Verify captured the moment a police officer confronted the suspect on the ground before discharging his weapon several times, after which the officer collapsed from bullet wounds. Two law enforcement officers sustained gunshot wounds during the arrest. Polish Interior Minister Marcin Kierwiński stated on X that one of the officers was shot three times and remains in very critical condition while undergoing emergency surgery.

"Police officers in Kraków risked their lives to intervene. Immediately. Thanks to their heroic actions, the attacker was quickly apprehended,"
Interior Minister Marcin Kierwiński wrote on social media platform X.

Police confirmed that the gunman was shot and wounded during the gunfight before being taken into custody and transported to a hospital under heavy guard. According to reporting by Polish news outlet Onet citing police sources, the suspect initially carried no identification documents, but law enforcement established his identity as a male Polish citizen through rapid operational work. Małopolska police confirmed that the man acted alone and that multiple additional firearms and large quantities of ammunition were secured inside his vehicle.

Casualties and Emergency Response in Krakow

The human toll of the attack quickly escalated throughout Saturday afternoon. The Małopolska regional government confirmed via social media that six people were killed in the assault, updating an earlier death toll of five. Authorities verified that the deceased victims include four women and two men. At least 15 additional individuals suffered injuries, ranging from gunshot wounds to trauma sustained during the stampede, and are receiving care at local hospitals.

Local resident Annette, a woman in her sixties, expressed shock over the violence when speaking to Agence France-Presse.

"I saw a man shooting at people and walking around the square,"
Annette told AFP.
"It's a tragedy for Krakow; we've never seen anything like this before."

In response to the active crisis, city officials issued urgent public safety alerts, urging residents and tourists to avoid the Main Market Square and surrounding central districts while forensics teams processed the extensive crime scene. Bullet marks damaged several storefronts across the historic square, leaving glass shards and debris scattered across the pavement.

Political Leadership and Global Reactions

The unprecedented attack in Poland—a NATO and European Union member state of 38 million people where mass shootings are extremely rare—prompted immediate reactions from political leaders domestically and internationally. Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk announced he was traveling directly from Warsaw to Krakow to manage the government's response alongside local leaders.

Writing on social media platform X, Prime Minister Tusk appealed for national unity and warned against political opportunism.

"My deepest condolences to the families and loved ones of the victims of the tragic attack in Kraków. The highest praise for the heroic police officers who risked their lives. And a heartfelt appeal to politicians of all stripes: do not exploit this tragedy. We will establish all the circumstances of this crime,"
Tusk wrote on X.

Polish President Karol Nawrocki also released a statement, expressing that he was "deeply concerned" by the attack and awaiting "a full explanation of the circumstances surrounding these tragic events."

International allies quickly voiced solidarity with Poland. United States Ambassador to Poland Tom Rose condemned the violence in a public statement.

"We are just learning details of the horrific mass shooting in Krakow, a savage and murderous attack that's shattered the peace of Poland,"
Rose wrote on X.
"America stands with Poland at this dark hour as Poland stands with her beloved citizens of Krakow."

European Union top diplomat Kaja Kallas also expressed profound sympathy on behalf of the EU.

"The tragic loss of life in the shooting in Kraków is a tragedy that will be felt far beyond Poland,"
Kallas stated on X.
"Poland's strength lies in the resilience of its people. In this moment of grief, Poland does not stand alone."

Security Protocols and What Happens Next in Krakow

As the immediate danger has been contained, police officials confirmed that no other suspects are being sought in connection with the attack. However, the broader investigation into the suspect's background, motive, and acquisition of military-grade firearms remains active. The incident has raised immediate questions regarding public safety in high-density urban areas, particularly around public spaces in Poland.

While Krakow is well-known for its vibrant tourism sector—ranging from medieval landmarks in the Old Town to popular leisure facilities like local krakow shooting range venues and historical tours—a violent rampage of this scale in public space is entirely unprecedented. Authorities have emphasized that security will be temporarily heightened across Krakow as federal and regional investigators determine how the sole gunman managed to assemble multiple military weapons and conduct the assault uninterrupted until police intervened.

In the coming days, Polish law enforcement will continue operational work to analyze the suspect's vehicle contents, review authenticated surveillance and phone footage, and provide updates on the condition of the injured police officer and hospitalised victims.
